About the platform

The platform is being developed as an information and queue-management solution for bus operations at Mulungushi Bus Station in Ndola. It aims to support station staff and drivers through clearer queue records and timely operational updates.

Core capabilities

A central place to manage bus registration, route queues and driver communication.

📷

Bus identification

An ESP32-CAM-based component is intended to capture bus registration plates to support identification and verification.

↕

Route queue management

Route-specific queues are organised using a First-In, First-Out (FIFO) approach, with queue positions updated after confirmed departures.

🔔

Queue information and alerts

Drivers can view waiting-bus counts for eligible routes, receive queue updates and see route suggestions based on queue length.

How it is intended to work

The proposed workflow supports a more structured process from bus arrival to departure.

STEP 01

Register

Driver and bus details are recorded and verified.

STEP 02

Identify

The camera captures the registration plate for identification.

STEP 03

Join a route queue

The driver reviews eligible route queue counts and selects a route.

STEP 04

Receive updates

Queue positions are updated and drivers are notified as their turn approaches.
