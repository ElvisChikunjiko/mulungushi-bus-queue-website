These draft terms describe intended use of the Mulungushi Bus Queue System. Review and adapt them before making the service available to users.

Purpose

The platform is intended to support bus registration, route-based queue management, queue information and operational notifications.

Accurate information

Users should provide accurate driver, bus and contact details and notify authorised station staff when information changes.

Queue operation

Queue positions and route information are system records intended to assist station operations. Authorised station staff remain responsible for confirming operational events, including bus departures and exceptions.

Availability

The platform may be unavailable during maintenance, network interruptions or technical faults. Users should follow instructions from authorised station staff where the system is unavailable.
